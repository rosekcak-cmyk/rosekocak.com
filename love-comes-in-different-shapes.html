Love Comes in Different Shapes

A community-engaged public art project that asked children to draw what love looked like to them, then translated selected drawings into illuminated sculptures shared with the city.

About the Project

What shape does love take?

Created for the month of Valentine's Day in 2019, Love Comes in Different Shapes began by moving away from the familiar commercial red-heart symbol. Children were invited to draw what love meant to them in their own visual language.

Kocak collected the drawings and translated selected images into illuminated sculptural forms. Flowers, figures, butterflies, animals and imagined shapes became glowing lines in public space, preserving the spontaneity of the children's marks while changing their scale and material.

The project treated public space as a place for shared imagination. By placing children's images into the city, the work proposed that love can be found everywhere, in everything and in many different shapes.
