About

Art as a shared experience.

Rose Kocak is a Turkish-American multidisciplinary public artist working across fiber, textile, installation and community-based art. Based between the cultural experiences of Türkiye and the United States, Kocak uses accessible materials and familiar visual languages to transform shared spaces into places of connection, play and collective expression.

Her public practice began in Boston with Love Comes in Different Shapes, a project that translated children’s drawings of love into illuminated sculptures presented in public space. The project established ideas that continue throughout her work: participation, belonging, childhood imagination and the possibility of art appearing outside traditional gallery walls.

Kocak later expanded her practice through crochet and yarn-based public interventions, community fiber installations, tufting, workshops and projects incorporating recycled studio materials, animal imagery and contemporary interpretations of Turkish and Ottoman visual traditions.

Her current practice focuses on collaborative and site-responsive public artworks that bring color, tactility and human connection into everyday environments.
