About

Art as a shared experience.

Rose Kocak is a Turkish-American multidisciplinary public artist working across fiber, textile, installation and community-based art. Her practice uses accessible materials and familiar visual languages to transform shared spaces into places of connection, play and collective expression.

Her public practice began in Boston with Love Comes in Different Shapes, a project that translated children's drawings of love into illuminated sculptures presented in public space. Participation, belonging, childhood imagination and the possibility of art outside traditional gallery walls continue throughout her work.

Her current practice focuses on collaborative and site-responsive public artworks that bring color, tactility and human connection into everyday environments.
